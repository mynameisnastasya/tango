Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Information we collect

When you submit an application, ABHI AGENCY may collect the contact and profile information you choose to provide, including your name, country, languages, social-media usernames, messaging contacts, live-streaming platform information, referral code, availability and introduction.

Age requirement

The application is intended only for people aged 18 or older.

How we use information

Information may be used to review applications, contact applicants, provide onboarding and agency support, administer referral attribution and understand recruitment performance.

Sensitive documents

Do not submit passports, government identity documents, banking credentials or passwords through the public application form.

Storage notice

The current GitHub Pages demo stores application data in the visitor's browser only. The production server version is designed to use a secured backend database.
